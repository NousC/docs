---
title: "List deliveries"
openapi: "GET /v2/webhooks/{id}/deliveries"
---

Recent sends for one webhook, newest first.

| `status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `delivered` | Your URL answered `2xx`. |
| `pending` | Queued or waiting for a retry. `next_attempt_at` says when. |
| `failed` | A permanent `4xx`, or six failed attempts. `last_status_code` and `last_error` say why. |
