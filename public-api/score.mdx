---
title: "Score"
openapi: "POST /v2/score"
---

Scores a lead against your ICP. Needs an ICP first. [Create one](/public-api/icp/create) or [train one from closed deals](/public-api/icp/train).

| Send | Scores | Writes |
|---|---|---|
| `identifier` | One lead in the graph | Keeps the score on the account |
| `identifiers` | Up to 100 graph leads | Keeps the scores |
| `attributes` | One lead not in Nous | Nothing |
| `leads` | Up to 1000 leads not in Nous | Nothing |

`icp` is the 0 to 100 fit and its tier. `intent` is how warm the account is now. `layered` splits the score into fit, pain, intent, and ability. A layer listed in `missing` is unknown, not zero.
