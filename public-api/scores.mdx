---
title: "Send your own scores"
openapi: "POST /v2/scores"
---

Writes scores you computed with your own system onto accounts. Each score becomes the account's ICP fit, so [`get_context`](/public-api/context), the accounts list, and tiers all read it. Every score is checked against the deals you win and lose, so you can see whether your model predicts revenue.

The workspace must use its own scorer first. See [Choose the scorer](/public-api/icp/scorer).

| Send | Writes |
|---|---|
| `identifier`, `score` | One account |
| `scores` | Up to 500 accounts |

`scorer` names your model, for example `acme-v3`. Change the name when you change the model, and the score history shows the switch.

Leave out `tier` and OpenNous derives it from the score and your [tier bands](/public-api/icp/tiers). Sending the same score again changes nothing. A new score keeps the old one in the account's history.

Accounts OpenNous has not seen come back as `unknown_identifier`. Record the account first, then send its score. The step-by-step guide is [Bring your own scorer](/getting-started/bring-your-own-scorer).
