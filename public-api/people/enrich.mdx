---
title: "Enrich a person"
openapi: "POST /v2/people/{id}/enrich"
---

Runs your enrichment providers on one person, then rescores it against your ICP. Connect an Apollo, Prospeo, or Findymail key in **Settings → Integrations** first.

Providers run in turn until one returns data. With a LinkedIn URL the order is Prospeo, Findymail, Apollo. Without one it is Apollo, Findymail, Prospeo.

A person enriched in the last 90 days comes back as is, with `cached: true` and nothing spent. Send `force: true` to enrich again.

The person needs an email, a LinkedIn URL, or a full name plus a company domain. Otherwise the call returns `422`.
