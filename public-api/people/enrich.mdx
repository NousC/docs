---
title: "Enrich a person"
openapi: "POST /v2/people/{id}/enrich"
---

Runs your connected enrichment providers on one person. Providers run in order Apollo, Prospeo, Findymail, and the first one that returns data wins. Connect at least one in **Settings → Integrations** first.

`fields_added` lists what changed. `enriched` is `false` when no provider found anything new.
