title: "Get a deal"
openapi: "GET /v2/deals/{id}"
---

One deal in full. Stakeholders with their role in the decision, stage history, activity, the objections, competitors, budget, and timeline filed to it, health, and win odds for an open deal.

An `implied:<account_id>` id on an account that has CRM deals returns `404`. Open one of the CRM deals instead.
