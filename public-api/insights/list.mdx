---
title: "List insights"
openapi: "GET /v2/insights"
---

What customers said about your product, sales, success, and market, tied to the account that said it. This is the same set the Insights board in the app shows. Only sorted insights linked to an account appear, newest first. Lines your own team said are left out.

Each insight has one `lane` (who has to act) and one `subtype` (what kind of work). The response carries the workspace's `taxonomy`, so you can label results without hardcoding it.

| Lane | Subtype | Label | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| `product` | `bug` | Bug | Something in the product is broken or fails. |
| `product` | `friction` | Friction | It works, but it is manual, slow, clunky, or confusing. |
| `product` | `feature` | Feature request | A capability you do not have. |
| `sales` | `objection` | Objection | A reason against buying, switching, or renewing, other than price. |
| `sales` | `pricing` | Pricing | Cost, fees, discounts, value for money. |
| `sales` | `pitch` | Pitch & messaging | The buyer misunderstood or questioned what you do or how you framed it. |
| `success` | `setup` | Setup | Getting it running. Install, integrations, logins, access. |
| `success` | `training` | Training & adoption | Staff not using it, or needing training or follow-up. |
| `market` | `partners` | Competitors & partners | Other vendors, incumbent tools, partners. Know, don't fix. |
| `market` | `buyers` | Who buys | Roles, decision makers, segments. Know, don't fix. |

Service businesses get service labels on the same keys. For example `bug` reads "Missed results" and `feature` reads "Service request". Read `taxonomy` for the labels your workspace uses.

New insights reach the board once they are sorted. Until then they do not appear here.
