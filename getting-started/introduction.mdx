## What is Nous?

Nous is **the GTM Context API**. It unifies fragmented data across your go-to-market tools like Apollo, HubSpot, Smartlead, Gmail, and LinkedIn into one unified customer graph to make agents context-aware about what is happening inside GTM.

- **Everything in one place.** Every event, every touchpoint, every conversation, saved in one place.
- **Built for the terminal.** Run GTM inside the terminal, plug into Claude Code, Codex, or your own agent via MCP.
- **25+ integrations.** Connects your GTM tools across CRMs, outbound, and LinkedIn.
- **Always in sync.** Keeps your outbound tools and CRM in sync.
- **A smarter ICP.** Build a sharper ICP model by analyzing what drives wins and losses over time.
