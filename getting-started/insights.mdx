---
title: "Insights"
description: "Read and write what customers said about your product, sales, success and market, over MCP and REST"
---

An insight is something a customer said about your business rather than about themselves: a bug, a feature request, an objection, a pricing concern, a competitor. Each one is tied to the account that said it, with the quote and speaker when there is one.

Insights are available through MCP and the REST API. There is no Insights page in the open-source app.

## Where insights come from

- **Extraction.** When the worker processes a call transcript, it runs a second pass that pulls out what the call taught you about your own business.
- **Your agent.** An agent that reads a call or email can write insights directly with `record_insight` or `POST /v2/insights`.

New insights are sorted once a day, around 05:30 UTC. Sorting places each one in a lane and subtype and links it to an account. Until then it does not appear in reads.

## Read insights

Over MCP, call [`get_insights`](/mcp/tools/get-insights). Over REST:

```bash
curl "https://api.opennous.cloud/v2/insights?lane=sales&subtype=objection&since=2026-09-01" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NOUS_API_KEY"
```

Filter by `lane`, `subtype`, one account with `focus`, or a start date with `since`. Lanes and subtypes are listed on [List insights](/public-api/insights/list). The response includes the workspace's `taxonomy`, so you can show labels without hardcoding them.

## Write insights

Over MCP, call `record_insight`. Over REST:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.opennous.cloud/v2/insights \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NOUS_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "source_label": "Acme discovery call",
    "occurred_at": "2026-10-01T15:00:00Z",
    "insights": [
      {
        "category": "buyer",
        "content": "Security review is owned by the CISO, not IT.",
        "quote": "Anything touching CRM data goes through our CISO.",
        "speaker": "VP Sales, Acme"
      }
    ]
  }'
```

`category` is one of `product`, `positioning`, `market` or `buyer`. Send every insight from one source in one call. Near-duplicates are skipped, so `written` can be lower than `submitted`. See [Record insights](/public-api/insights/record).

Facts about the account itself, such as a new title or a budget, are not insights. Write those as a [note](/public-api/notes).

On a self-hosted instance, replace `https://api.opennous.cloud` with your own API URL.
