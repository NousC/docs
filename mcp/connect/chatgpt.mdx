---
title: "ChatGPT"
description: "Add OpenNous to ChatGPT as a custom connector."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on developer mode">
    In ChatGPT, go to **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings** and turn on **Developer mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Back in **Apps & Connectors**, click **Create**. Name it `OpenNous`, paste the URL and set authentication to **OAuth**.

    ```
    https://mcp.opennous.cloud/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Create**, sign in to OpenNous and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In a new chat, turn on OpenNous from the tools menu and ask:

> Which deals went quiet this week?

ChatGPT sees only what your OpenNous user can see. Revoke it in **Settings → API keys** in OpenNous.

<Note>On Business and Enterprise, an admin may need to allow custom connectors.</Note>
