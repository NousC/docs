---
title: "Grok"
description: "Add OpenNous to Grok as a custom connector."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open connectors">
    Go to [grok.com/connectors](https://grok.com/connectors), click **New connector**, then **Custom**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL">
    Name it `OpenNous` and paste:

    ```
    https://mcp.opennous.cloud/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in to OpenNous and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then ask Grok:

> Prep me for the next call with Acme.

Grok sees only what your OpenNous user can see. Revoke it in **Settings → API keys** in OpenNous.

<Note>On Grok Business and Enterprise, a team admin adds the connector first.</Note>
