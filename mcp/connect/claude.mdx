---
title: "Claude"
description: "Install the OpenNous plugin in Claude.ai and the Claude desktop app."
---

The OpenNous plugin gives Claude the skills for revenue work: focus your day, brief before a call, write the follow-up, forecast and review the pipeline. It also connects Claude to your OpenNous workspace, so every answer comes from your deals, customers and calls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the plugin">
    [Download the OpenNous plugin](https://www.opennous.cloud/downloads/opennous-plugin.zip). In Claude, open **Customize → Plugins**, click **Add → Upload plugin** and choose the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type /login in a chat">
    Open a new chat and type `/login`. Click **Connect**, sign in to OpenNous and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get started">
    On a new workspace, Claude sets it up from your history. Otherwise it shows what needs you today. Then ask anything:

    > Brief me before my call with Acme.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Claude sees only what your OpenNous user can see. Revoke it in **Settings → API keys** in OpenNous, where it shows as a connected app.

<Note>On Team and Enterprise, an owner can install the plugin once for the whole organization.</Note>

## Only the connector

To give Claude the OpenNous tools without the skills, go to **Customize → Connectors → Add → Add custom connector**, name it `OpenNous` and paste `https://mcp.opennous.cloud/mcp`.
