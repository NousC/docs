---
title: "Claude"
description: "Add the OpenNous revenue plugin to Claude.ai and the Claude desktop app."
---

The revenue plugin gives Claude the skills for revenue work: focus your day, brief before a call, write the follow-up, forecast and review the pipeline. The OpenNous connector gives those skills your deals, customers and calls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the revenue plugin">
    In Claude, open **Customize → Plugins**, click **Add marketplace** and enter:

    ```
    NousC/revenue-plugin
    ```

    Then install **OpenNous**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the OpenNous connector">
    Open **Customize → Connectors**, click **Add → Add custom connector**. Name it `OpenNous`, paste the URL and click **Continue**:

    ```
    https://mcp.opennous.cloud/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Connect**, sign in to OpenNous and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    > What should I focus on today?

    Claude picks the right skill and answers from OpenNous.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Claude sees only what your OpenNous user can see. Revoke it in **Settings → API keys** in OpenNous, where it shows as a connected app.

<Note>On Team and Enterprise, an owner may need to allow custom connectors and plugins first.</Note>
