---
title: "score"
description: "Score leads against your ICP."
---

Pass one of `identifier`, `identifiers`, `attributes`, or `leads`. Leads in the graph keep their score. Leads scored from attributes are scored inline and nothing is written.

| Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `identifier` | string | One lead in the graph. Email, domain, LinkedIn URL, or entity UUID. |
| `identifiers` | string[] | Up to 100 graph leads |
| `attributes` | object | One lead not in OpenNous. `job_title`, `seniority`, `industry`, `employee_count`, `company_type`, `location`, `keywords` or `headline`. |
| `leads` | object[] | Up to 1000 leads not in OpenNous, each an `attributes` object. Add `ref` to match rows. |
| `intent` | string | Reserved. Accepted and ignored today. |
| `scorer` | string | Your model's name, such as `acme-v3`. Use it with `scores` to send your own scores |
| `scores` | object[] | Up to 500 of `{ identifier, score, tier?, reason? }` from your own scorer |

```
sarah@acme.com — ICP 82/100 (tier 1) ✓fit · intent 64/100 Warm
    layers: fit 82 · pain 70 · intent 64
    unknown: ability — work now — high priority
    VP-level buyer, 200-500 employees, B2B SaaS
```

A layer listed as unknown has no evidence yet. It is not zero.

## Send your own scores

Pass `scorer` and `scores` to write scores you computed yourself instead of reading OpenNous's. The workspace must [use its own scorer](/public-api/icp/scorer). Each row reports `staked`, `updated`, or `unchanged`. An account OpenNous has not seen returns `unknown_identifier`, so record it first. Leave out `tier` and it is derived from your [tier bands](/public-api/icp/tiers).

REST: [`POST /v2/score`](/public-api/score). Your own scores go to [`POST /v2/scores`](/public-api/scores).
