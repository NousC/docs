---
title: Salesforce
description: Connect Salesforce to sync Contacts, Accounts, and Opportunities into Proply via OAuth.
---

Proply connects to Salesforce with OAuth 2.0 (PKCE). After authorizing, Proply can search and import **Contacts**, **Accounts**, and **Opportunities** from your Salesforce org and pull the associated contact emails for every opportunity.

## Environment variables

Add these to `proply.env`:

```
SALESFORCE_CLIENT_ID=
SALESFORCE_CLIENT_SECRET=
SALESFORCE_REDIRECT_URI=https://api.yourdomain.com/api/workflow-providers/salesforce/oauth/callback
SALESFORCE_LOGIN_URL=https://login.salesforce.com
```

<Note>
For a Salesforce **sandbox**, set `SALESFORCE_LOGIN_URL=https://test.salesforce.com`.
</Note>

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Connected App in Salesforce">
    In Salesforce go to **Setup → App Manager → New Connected App**.

    Fill out:
    - **Connected App Name**: `Proply`
    - **API Name**: `Proply`
    - **Contact Email**: your email

    Under **API (Enable OAuth Settings)**:
    - Check **Enable OAuth Settings**
    - **Callback URL**: paste your `SALESFORCE_REDIRECT_URI` (e.g. `https://api.yourdomain.com/api/workflow-providers/salesforce/oauth/callback`)
    - **Selected OAuth Scopes**: add
      - `Manage user data via APIs (api)`
      - `Perform requests at any time (refresh_token, offline_access)`
    - Check **Require Proof Key for Code Exchange (PKCE)**
    - Check **Require Secret for Web Server Flow**

    Save. Salesforce takes ~10 minutes to provision the app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grab the Consumer Key and Secret">
    Open the Connected App you just created and click **Manage Consumer Details** (you may be re-prompted for your password).

    Copy:
    - **Consumer Key** → `SALESFORCE_CLIENT_ID`
    - **Consumer Secret** → `SALESFORCE_CLIENT_SECRET`

    Paste both into your `proply.env` and restart the API:
    ```bash
    docker compose restart api
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect in Proply">
    In Proply go to **Mind → Integrations → Available → Salesforce** (or **Settings → Integrations → + Add Integration → Salesforce**) and click **Connect Salesforce via OAuth**.

    A Salesforce login window opens. Approve the requested scopes. The window closes and Salesforce appears under **Connected**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sync your data">
    Open the Salesforce card → **Configure sync**. From here you can:
    - Run an on-demand import of Contacts, Accounts, and Opportunities
    - Enable **Auto-sync daily** to keep Proply current

    Opportunities pulled in are logged as `deal_created` / `deal_won` activity on every related contact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="`salesforce_not_configured` when clicking Connect">
    Proply could not find `SALESFORCE_CLIENT_ID` or `SALESFORCE_REDIRECT_URI` in `proply.env`. Add them and restart the API.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="`redirect_uri_mismatch` on the Salesforce login screen">
    The **Callback URL** in your Connected App must match `SALESFORCE_REDIRECT_URI` **exactly** (including scheme, domain, port, and path). One trailing slash or mismatched subdomain will fail.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="`Salesforce 401 — token expired or revoked`">
    Disconnect Salesforce in Proply and reconnect. Refresh-token rotation requires the `refresh_token, offline_access` scope to be enabled on the Connected App.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Connecting to a Salesforce sandbox">
    Set `SALESFORCE_LOGIN_URL=https://test.salesforce.com` in `proply.env` and restart the API. Sandboxes use the same Connected App config but a different login host.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
