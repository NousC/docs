---
title: "Providers"
description: "Connect your GTM stack to Nous"
---

Nous connects to your existing GTM tools to ingest relationship signals. Every event, email sent, LinkedIn message, meeting held, website visit, flows into the unified context graph automatically.

<Note>
  All providers are connected per workspace under **Integrations** in the app.
</Note>

## Signal providers

Real-time signals from your communication, outbound, and meeting tools.
